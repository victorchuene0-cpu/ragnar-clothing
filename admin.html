Delivery

The fee is added to the bag total, so customers see the real price before they message you. Leave it empty if it varies: the bag then says "to be confirmed" and you tell them in WhatsApp.

Returns & refunds

Starter wording follows South Africa's rules for online sales (ECTA s44: 7 days to cancel, refund within 30 days; made-to-order items are excluded). It is a starting point, not legal advice. Edit it so it is true for your business and have it checked.

Contact

South African online-trading law (ECTA s43) expects a shop website to show who is selling and how to reach them. Fill in what applies to you.

Privacy

Starter wording describes how this site really works: it stores no customer details, and delivery details stay on the customer's phone and reach you by WhatsApp. Change it if you do anything more with customers' details.
